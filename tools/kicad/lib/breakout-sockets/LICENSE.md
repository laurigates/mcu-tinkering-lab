from two kinds of third-party material. Both are share-alike licences, so the
footprints are shared under the same terms and keep this attribution.

board file, as recorded in the breakout's page under `docs/reference/boards/`

## Pad shape, drill and courtyard margin: the KiCad library

Each pad copies pad 1 (rect) or pad 2 (circle) of
`Connector_PinSocket_2.54mm:PinSocket_1x08_P2.54mm_Vertical` from the KiCad
10.0.6 footprint library, and the courtyard and silkscreen margins are that
footprint's. The KiCad libraries are licensed under
[CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/legalcode), with
the design-use exception quoted in `../kicad-stock/LICENSE.md`.

## Use in a design

The KiCad exception covers designs that use the KiCad-derived part. The
Adafruit and SparkFun licences carry no such exception; whether placing these
footprints on a board makes that board an adaptation of the vendor's design is
not settled here. Keep this attribution with any redistribution of the
footprints, and credit the breakout vendors in a published board's notes.
