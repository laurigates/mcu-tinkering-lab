# Copper-foil test board

One single-layer board, knife-cut from adhesive copper foil on a Silhouette
Portrait 2, that decides whether foil is worth laying real carriers out for
([PRD-013](../../requirements/PRD-013-rapid-hardware-prototyping.md), FR-022).
Nothing about foil is assumed to work until this board has been built and the
results table below is filled in.

| File | What it is |
|---|---|
| [`copper.svg`](copper.svg) | Cut file for the copper foil: every island, the 70 × 60 mm board outline, and three spare bridge strips to the right |
| [`kapton.svg`](kapton.svg) | Cut file for Kapton: three crossover patches |
| [`guide.svg`](guide.svg) | The board filled and labelled, with a 50 mm scale bar. Print it at 100 % to check scale and to find each test |

All three are generated by [`tools/foil-test-board.py`](../../../tools/foil-test-board.py);
edit the script, not the SVGs, and regenerate with
`python3 tools/foil-test-board.py`. Its tests (`python3 -m unittest
tools/test_foil_test_board.py`) check that each gap, width and clearance is
what the board claims to test.

## What is on it

| Test | Geometry | Question |
|---|---|---|
| Width ladder | 24 mm traces of 0.6, 0.8, 1.0, 1.27, 1.5, 2.0 mm, 3 mm pads at both ends | The narrowest trace that survives weeding and transfer |
| Gap ladder | Pairs of 1 mm traces 0.4, 0.6, 0.8, 1.0, 1.27 mm apart | The smallest gap that stays open |
| Corners | 0.8 mm L-traces with a 90° corner, a 45° chamfer and a 2 mm fillet | Which corner tears when weeded |
| SMD socket | The pads of KiCad's `PinSocket_1x08_P2.54mm_Vertical_SMD` (1.0 × 1.9 mm, alternating ±1.65 mm) | Does a socket stay put while a module is plugged and pulled |
| Power | 3 mm trace, 5 mm pads | Does the adhesive survive 20 AWG leads and a few amps |
| Crossover | A 1 mm trace, crossed by two stubs whose pads stop 1 mm from it | Does a copper bridge on a Kapton patch join the stubs without touching the trace |

## Materials

- Adhesive copper foil, A4 sheet or wide tape, 18–35 µm. The research
  behind PRD-013 found 3M 1181 and 1126 used for this (MIT, MakeHaven);
  generic A4 shielding foil is untested here
- Kapton (polyimide) tape, 25–50 µm
- Medium-tack transfer tape
- A blank 70 × 60 mm substrate: single-sided FR4 with the copper removed, or
  any stiff board
- Flux, a fine iron tip, one SMD 1×8 2.54 mm female header, 20 AWG wire

## Build

1. **Cut `copper.svg`** with
   [inkscape-silhouette](https://github.com/fablabnbg/inkscape-silhouette),
   which lists the Portrait 2 as confirmed working. On macOS:
   `brew install libusb`, then its `install_osx.sh`. Blade settings for
   copper foil on the Portrait 2 are not known; cut a 10 mm test square first
   and raise the force until the foil parts and the liner only scores. Note
   the settings that worked in the table below.
2. **Transfer before weeding.** Lay transfer tape over the whole cut sheet,
   lift it, and put the foil down on the substrate. Burnish firmly, then peel
   the transfer tape and weed the background away from the traces, pulling
   toward each trace's end, never across it.
3. **Cut `kapton.svg`** and stick one patch across the through trace between
   the two crossover stub pads. Its edges stop 0.2 mm short of each pad.
4. **Bridge:** lay a spare bridge strip from `copper.svg` over the patch, end to
   end across both stub pads, and tack-solder each end. Keep the iron on for
   under 2 s: the foil's acrylic adhesive softens with heat.
5. **Socket:** solder the SMD header onto the socket pads, pin 1 on the top
   row's left pad. Hold it down while the joints cool.
6. **Power:** solder 20 AWG leads to both 5 mm pads.

## Checks and results

Fill this in after building, then copy the outcome into PRD-013 as
measurements.

| Check | How | Pass if | Result |
|---|---|---|---|
| Cutter settings | — | — | blade, force, speed, passes: |
| Width ladder | Inspect after weeding; continuity end to end | Trace whole and beeps | narrowest that passed: |
| Gap ladder | Continuity across each pair | No beep | smallest that passed: |
| Corners | Inspect after weeding | No lift or tear | 90° / 45° / fillet: |
| Socket | Plug and pull a module 10 times; then continuity pad to pin | Every pad still attached and beeps | |
| Power | 3 A from a bench supply for 5 minutes; feel or measure the trace | Adhesive holds, no lift at the pads | temperature / drop: |
| Crossover | Continuity stub to stub, and each stub to the through trace | Stubs beep to each other, never to the trace | |
| Heat | Re-touch one joint per test with the iron for 3 s | Nothing lifts | |

**Decision:** foil is worth laying a real carrier out for if 0.8 mm traces and
0.6 mm gaps pass, the socket survives its 10 cycles, and the crossover
isolates. A carrier would still need its own single-layer layout with wide
tracks; the routed carrier boards cannot be cut as they are (PRD-013,
Findings).
