# 0.91-inch 128x32 SSD1306 I2C OLED Module

Four-pin I2C OLED module on robocar-unified, behind the TCA9548A on channel 1
(`I2C_BUS_CHANNEL_OLED` in `packages/robocar/unified/main/pin_config.h`). A
single row of four pins on one short edge: ground, supply, clock and data. For
the controller itself, and the 128x32 initialisation values, see
[`display--ssd1306.md`](../datasheets/display--ssd1306.md).

## Physical layout

Viewed from the display side with the header on the left short edge and the
panel's flex tail at the right end, as in listing photo 2 (the wiring diagram).
The silkscreen reads bottom to top in that view, `GND` nearest the bottom
corner. The header pins exit through the back of the board (photos 1, 5 and 6).

`Side`/`Pos` follow the convention in
[`tools/hardware/layout.py`](../../../tools/hardware/layout.py): `L`/`R`/`T`/`B`
edge, `Pos 1` at the top of a left or right edge. There is no vendor board file,
so `Header` counts the pads from the square one, which photo 2 shows at `GND`.
`Pin` uses the bus names the join wires a mux channel to (`SDA`, `SCL`); the
board prints the clock as `SCK`, kept in `Silkscreen`.

| Pin | Side | Pos | Header | Silkscreen | Notes |
|-----|------|-----|--------|------------|-------|
| SDA | L | 1 | 4 | `SDA` | I2C data |
| SCL | L | 2 | 3 | `SCK` | I2C clock |
| VCC | L | 3 | 2 | `VCC` | Module supply. The listing's wiring diagram feeds it 5 V; robocar-unified feeds it 3V3 |
| GND | L | 4 | 1 | `GND` | Square pad |

**Pitch and outline:** not given. The listing states no dimensions and the
photos carry no scale. The header looks like a standard 0.1-inch row, but that
is a reading of a photo, not a measurement. The closest published panel for this
size, Univision's UG-2832HSWEG02, is 30.0 × 11.50 × 1.45 mm
(UG-2832HSWEG02 spec §1.2, p. 1); that is the glass, not this PCB, and nothing
in the listing confirms that panel is the one fitted.

## Source

Read from the listing's silkscreen photos, not from a vendor board file — none is
published for this module. **The pin order (GND, VCC, SCL, SDA from the square
pad end) was verified by Lauri on the fitted module on 2026-10-16.** Nothing
else on this page has been checked on the board.
`.claude/rules/board-layout-from-vendor-files.md` does not accept a product photo
as authoritative: silkscreen labels are legible, but a mirrored or rotated read
looks just as correct. Treat the rest of the page as unconfirmed until the
checks below are done on the fitted board.

Photos used (listing gallery order, 2026-10-14):

- Photo 2, the wiring diagram: the clearest view of the header silkscreen, the
  square `GND` pad, and the flex tail at the far end.
- Photos 1 and 3–6: the same silkscreen order from the display side at an
  angle.
- Photo 6: the back, with `0.91 OLED` and the designators above. No address
  jumper is visible on either face.

The listing's wiring diagram (photo 2) runs `SDA` to an Arduino Nano's `A5`
and `SCK` to `A4`. On a Nano `A4` is SDA and `A5` is SCL, so the diagram has
the bus crossed. Do not wire from it.

A 0.91-inch module whose silkscreen order differs is a different board and needs
its own page.

## Check on the physical board before a carrier is ordered

| Check | How | Why |
|-------|-----|-----|
| Pin order | **Done:** verified by Lauri on the fitted module, 2026-10-16 | The order above was a photo read |
| Square pad | **Done:** Lauri verified the order GND, VCC, SCL, SDA from the square-pad end, 2026-10-16 | Fixes the `Header` numbering |
| Header pitch | Calipers across the outer two pins (3 × pitch) | Not stated anywhere in the listing |
| Outline and header offset | Calipers: PCB length and width, header row distance from the short edge, mounting holes if any | The carrier footprint needs them; the photos have no scale |
| Pin exit side | Confirm the pins leave through the back, display facing away from the carrier | Decides whether the carrier footprint is mirrored |
| I2C address | Run `robocar-bringup`; its `i2c-scan` check lists what ACKs on channel 1 | The firmware assumes `0x3C`; the SSD1306 answers `0x3D` instead when SA0 is high (SSD1306 datasheet §8.1.5, p. 19) and no strap is visible |
| Supply path | Read the marking on `Q1`; with `VCC` on the 3V3 rail, measure the voltage at the panel side of it | If `Q1` is a regulator, a 3.3 V input leaves the panel below the 3.3–4.2 V its charge pump needs (UG-2832HSWEG02 spec §3.2, p. 7) |
| On-board pull-ups | Unpowered, measure `SDA`→`VCC` and `SCK`→`VCC` | They parallel the bus's other pull-ups; the SSD1306 allows 300 ns rise time (datasheet Table 13-6, p. 54) |
| 32 rows | Run the panel with the 128x32 initialisation (`A8h 1Fh`, `DAh 02h`) and check text renders at full height | Confirms the panel geometry the listing claims |
